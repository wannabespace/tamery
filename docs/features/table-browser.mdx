Open a connection and pick a table in the Navigator. A single click previews it in a reusable tab. Double-click to keep the tab open.

## Find the rows you need

Type in the filter field to pick a column, an operator, and a value. Values are suggested as you go. On a foreign key column you can also filter by the related table's columns, like orders whose customer lives in Berlin.

Faster still: right-click a cell and choose **Filter by Value**. On a null cell it adds an `IS NULL` filter.

Or describe it. Type “unpaid orders from last week” and pick **Ask AI**. It replaces your filters and may set a sort too, so glance at what it built. Free accounts get 20 of these a week; [Pro](/account/plans) has no limit.

You can switch a filter off without deleting it. Useful when you are hunting for the one that hides everything.

Click a column header to sort ascending, again for descending, a third time to stop. Shift-click another header to sort by it as well.

A related cell has a small arrow. Click it to peek at the record it points to, or at the rows that point back. **Open in Table** opens those rows in their own tab.

The row count may be an estimate on large tables. Click it to get the exact number.

Filters, sorting, and the column layout are remembered per table on this device.

## Edit rows

1. Double-click a cell, press **Enter**, or just start typing.
2. Change the value and press **Enter**. The edit becomes a draft.
3. Click **Save** to write your drafts to the database. **Review** shows them first.

To throw drafts away, open **Review** and choose **Discard all**, or right-click a cell and choose **Discard Change**. **Cmd+Z** (**Ctrl+Z**) undoes the last change and **Cmd+Shift+Z** (**Ctrl+Shift+Z**) brings it back.

Use the toolbar's **+ → Add row** for a new row, or right-click a cell and choose **Duplicate Row**. To drop a new row before saving, click **Discard row** where its checkbox would be.

To delete saved rows, tick their checkboxes and click **Delete**, or right-click a cell and choose **Delete Row** for just that one. Tamery asks before anything goes.

A few more things that save time:

- Paste a block from a spreadsheet, or copy cells back into one.
- Select several cells, edit one, and press **Cmd+Enter** (**Ctrl+Enter**) to write that value into all of them.
- **Fill Down** copies the top value of a selection into the cells below it.
- **Set Null** clears a value to `NULL`.

Without a primary key, Tamery finds a row by a unique key with no nullable columns, or else by its values. When an edit or delete would reach more rows than you picked, it tells you how many first. Adding rows still needs a primary key, and generated columns are read-only.

Changing filters or sorting clears drafted cell edits, so save them first. If you forget, undo brings them back. New rows you have not saved yet stay.

## Choose how data looks

**Cmd+Shift+E** (**Ctrl+Shift+E**) switches to the documents view, where each row is a card with expandable JSON. Press it again for the grid.

In the grid, drag column edges to resize and headers to reorder. The column menu hides or pins a column and resets widths or order.

**Distinct Values** in the column menu shows the most common values with their counts, plus the minimum and maximum. Click a value to filter by it.

A foreign key column shows a readable name from the related table as a tag after each ID, so you know who customer `42` is without opening another tab. Tamery picks a column like `name` or `email` on its own. **Show Labels** in the column menu picks another one or turns it off.

A time stored with a different time zone than yours shows the stored value, then your local time.

Press **Space** on a cell to peek at its full value.

## Change the structure

Use **+** in the toolbar or the header to add a column. Right-click a column to edit it, or a table in the Navigator to rename or drop it. See [Navigator and tabs](/features/navigator) for creating tables, views, and schemas.

What you can change depends on the database engine. For changes across several tables, use the [schema visualizer](/features/schema-visualizer).

## Generate test data

Choose **⋯ → Seed data**, check the generator Tamery picked for each column, choose how many rows you want, and insert. This writes real rows to your database, so point it at the right one.

One run inserts up to 10,000 rows. Free accounts get 10 runs on each device. [Pro](/account/plans) removes the limit.

To get rows out of a table, see [Export and code](/features/export).
