Open **Schema → Visualizer** in the Navigator.

Each card is a table or view. Lines connect foreign keys to the keys they point at. Click a card to see its columns, notes, relations, indexes, triggers, and policies. **Notes** holds what the AI should know about the table or its columns; see [Schema notes](/features/ai-assistant#schema-notes).

## Explore

Drag the background to pan and zoom to see more or less. **Cmd+F** (**Ctrl+F**) finds a table or column. Pick another schema in the toolbar to see its tables.

Drag tables wherever they make sense to you. Tamery remembers the layout on this device. **Arrange tables automatically** tidies everything up when it gets out of hand.

## Make changes

- **+ Table** creates a table.
- Right-click a table to rename it, add a column, open it, or drop it. On PostgreSQL, **Drop Table with Dependents** also removes what depends on it.
- Right-click a column to edit or drop it, or flip **Allow NULL** and **Require a Value**.
- To add a foreign key, drag a column's edge handle onto a primary or unique key. Or right-click the column and use **Reference**.
- To remove a foreign key, select the line and press **Delete**, or right-click it and choose **Drop Foreign Key**.

Foreign keys can be added on PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, and SQL Server. Views are read-only in the diagram.

## Review and apply

Changes stay as drafts until you click **Apply**, or press **Cmd+S** (**Ctrl+S**). The button counts them.

The eye button next to it shows each change with its exact SQL, and lets you discard any of them. Changed your mind about one table, column, or foreign key? Right-click it and choose **Restore Table**, **Restore Column**, or **Restore Foreign Key**. Dropping anything opens the review first, so nothing disappears by a misclick.

Drafts live in this window only. Reloading throws them away.

PostgreSQL, SQL Server, SQLite, Turso, and D1 apply all changes in one transaction: either everything lands or nothing does. MySQL, MariaDB, Oracle, and ClickHouse apply schema changes one by one, so an error can leave some of them already applied. Check what is left in the review before you try again.
