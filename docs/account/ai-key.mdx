Add an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter, and the [AI chat](/features/ai-assistant), dictation included, runs on that account. Your provider bills you for it directly. The chat then works on Free as well as Pro and doesn't spend the included $5 or your [AI credits](/account/ai-credits).

The key is for the chat only. AI edits and fixes, completions in the Query editor, AI filters and connection diagnosis keep running on Tamery, with your plan's limits.

## Add a key

1. Open **Settings** with the gear in the title bar, or press **Cmd+,** (**Ctrl+,**).
2. Pick **AI**.
3. Choose the **Provider** and paste the **API key**.
4. Click **Save**. Tamery sends the provider one short request to check the key, billed to it, and saves it if the provider accepts it.

The key is stored encrypted with your account, so it works on every device you sign in on. Settings shows only its last 4 characters. To stop using it, click **Remove**; the chat goes back to your plan.

## What changes with a key

|  | With your key |
| --- | --- |
| Models | The chat's model picker lists your provider's models, or every model with an OpenRouter key. **Auto** uses Claude Sonnet, GPT-6.1 Sol or Gemini 4 Argon; with an OpenRouter key it picks a model for each message, as without a key. |
| Dictation | Works with a Google, OpenAI or OpenRouter key; Anthropic keys can't transcribe audio. **Dictation model** in **Settings → AI** picks the model on this device: GPT-4o Transcribe (the default), GPT-4o mini Transcribe or Whisper on OpenAI, Gemini 3.8 Flash (the default) or Gemini 4 Argon on Google and OpenRouter. |
| Cost | [Usage](/account/ai-credits#usage) in **Settings → AI** lists chat calls made with your key on their own row per month, marked **Your key**, with their tokens and a cost estimated from the model's list price. Your provider's bill is what you pay. |
