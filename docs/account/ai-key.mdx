Connect Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter with an API key, one or several of them, and the [AI chat](/features/ai-assistant), dictation included, runs on those accounts. Your providers bill you for it directly. The chat then works on Free as well as Pro and doesn't spend the included $5 or your [AI credits](/account/ai-credits).

The keys are for the chat only. AI edits and fixes, completions in the Query editor, AI filters and connection diagnosis keep running on Tamery, with your plan's limits.

## Connect a provider

1. Pick **Settings** in the avatar menu, or press **Cmd+,** (**Ctrl+,**).
2. Pick **AI**.
3. Under **Your providers**, click **Add provider** (the **+** once you have one) and pick the provider.
4. Paste the **API key** and click **Connect**. Tamery sends the provider one short request to check the key, billed to it, and saves it if the provider accepts it.

Keys are stored encrypted with your account, so they work on every device you sign in on. Settings shows only a key's last 4 characters. Each provider holds one key: **Replace key** in its **⋯** menu changes it and **Remove** deletes it. The switch turns a provider off without removing its key. With every provider off or removed, the chat goes back to your plan.

## Several providers

A model you pick runs on its own provider's key, or on OpenRouter's when that provider isn't connected. **Auto**, chat titles, suggestions, compaction, web search and the check **Auto** approvals run before a change use the first provider that's on, in the order you connected them.

## What changes with a key

|  | With your key |
| --- | --- |
| Models | The chat's model picker lists your providers' models, or every model with an OpenRouter key. **Auto** uses Claude Sonnet, GPT-6.1 Sol or Gemini 4 Argon; with an OpenRouter key it picks a model for each message, as without a key. |
| Dictation | Works with a Google, OpenAI or OpenRouter key; Anthropic keys can't transcribe audio. **Dictation model** in **Settings → AI** picks the model on this device: GPT-4o Transcribe (the default), GPT-4o mini Transcribe or Whisper on OpenAI, Gemini 3.8 Flash (the default) or Gemini 4 Argon on Google and OpenRouter. Until you pick one, it's the default of the first provider that can transcribe. |
| Cost | [Usage](/account/ai-credits#usage) in **Settings → AI** lists chat calls made with your key on their own row per month, marked **Your key**, with their tokens and a cost estimated from the model's list price. Your providers' bills are what you pay. |
