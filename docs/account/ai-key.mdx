Add an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter, and every AI feature runs on that account: the chat, dictation, AI edits and fixes, completions in the Query editor, AI filters and connection diagnosis. Your provider bills you for it directly. Tamery's weekly limits, the included $5 and [AI credits](/account/ai-credits) no longer apply, and it works on Free as well as Pro.

## Add a key

1. Open **Settings** with the gear in the title bar, or press **Cmd+,** (**Ctrl+,**).
2. Pick **AI**.
3. Choose the **Provider** and paste the **API key**.
4. Click **Save**. Tamery sends the provider one short request to check the key, billed to it, and saves it if the provider accepts it.

The key is stored encrypted with your account, so it works on every device you sign in on. Settings shows only its last 4 characters. To stop using it, click **Remove**; AI goes back to your plan.

## What changes with a key

|  | With your key |
| --- | --- |
| Models | The chat's model picker lists your provider's models, or every model with an OpenRouter key. **Auto** uses Claude Sonnet, GPT-6.1 Sol or Gemini Pro; with an OpenRouter key it picks a model for each message, as without a key. |
| Dictation | Needs a Google or OpenRouter key. Anthropic and OpenAI keys can't transcribe audio. |
| Completions | Use the fast model on your key instead of Codestral. |
| Cost | [AI usage](/account/ai-usage) doesn't list calls made with your key. Check your provider's dashboard. |
