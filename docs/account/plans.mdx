## Free

- Unlimited connections, synced across your devices.
- Table browsing and editing, the Query editor, and schema tools.
- One personal workspace.
- Up to **3 shared table links** at a time. Expired links don't count.
- **20 AI filters** and **100 MCP queries** a week, reset on Monday at **00:00 UTC**.
- **Explain with AI** for connection errors, as often as you need it.
- **10 runs** of test-data generation on each device.

## Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- The AI assistant, plus AI edits, fixes, and completions in the Query editor.
- No limits on AI filters, MCP queries, or test-data generation.
- As many workspaces as you need.
- Unlimited shared table links.

Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. Pay monthly or yearly; prices are on the [pricing page](https://tamery.app/home#pricing). Start the upgrade from your [account](https://tamery.app/account).

## Billing

Your subscription is on the [account page](https://tamery.app/account). **Manage** there opens the billing portal, where you change your payment method or cancel.

[Billing](https://tamery.app/account/billing) lists your invoices. **Manage billing** opens the same portal.

If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

For Enterprise pricing or help with a deployment, use **Contact us** on the [pricing page](https://tamery.app/home#pricing).
