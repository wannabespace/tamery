## Free

- Unlimited connections, synced across your devices.
- Table browsing and editing, the Query editor, and schema tools.
- One personal workspace.
- Up to **3 shared table links** at a time. Expired links don't count.
- **20 AI filters** and **100 MCP queries** a week, reset on Monday at **00:00 UTC**.
- **Explain with AI** for connection errors, as often as you need it.
- The AI chat, when you add [your own AI key](/account/ai-key).
- **10 runs** of test-data generation on each device.

## Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- The AI assistant, plus AI edits, fixes, and completions in the Query editor. Each month includes **$5 of AI**, shared by every AI feature; [AI credits](/account/ai-credits) pay for more.
- No weekly limits on AI filters, MCP queries, or test-data generation.
- As many workspaces as you need.
- Unlimited shared table links.

Pro starts with a 7-day free trial, once per account. Pay monthly or yearly; prices are on the [pricing page](https://tamery.app/home#pricing). Start the upgrade in **Settings → Plan**: pick monthly or yearly and click **Upgrade** next to the plan. Checkout opens in your browser.

## Team

Pro for several people on one subscription. You pay per seat, choose how many at checkout, and take one of them yourself. Team starts with the same 7-day free trial, if your account hasn't had one. During the trial you can give one seat besides your own; the rest of the seats you chose open once the plan is paid.

A seat belongs to an email address, not to a [workspace](/features/workspaces). People you invite to a workspace without a seat stay on Free, and a seat gives its holder Pro only while your Team plan is active.

You can have one plan at a time. To change it, use the billing portal instead of a second checkout.

### Assign a seat

1. In **Settings → Plan**, find **Seats**.
2. Enter the person's email and click **Assign**.
3. They get an email and have Pro once they sign in with that address. It has to be verified. Gmail addresses match without dots or a `+` suffix, so `jane.doe+work@gmail.com` and `janedoe@gmail.com` are one seat.

If the email doesn't go out, the seat is still assigned: ask them to sign in with that address. Seat emails are limited per day. Assigning an email that already has a seat sends nothing and doesn't count toward the limit.

If a payment is past due, update your payment method and wait for the payment to go through before assigning more seats. Existing seats stay assigned while your Team is past due.

To give the seat to someone else, click **Remove** on their row and confirm, then assign it again. They lose Pro right away.

**Change seats** opens the billing portal, where you set how many you pay for. If you lower the count, the people you added last lose their seats.

To move from Pro, click **Switch to Team** in **Settings → Plan**. Moving back to Pro in the billing portal removes everyone's seats.

Changing your plan or seat count in the billing portal during a trial ends the trial, and you're charged right away.

## Billing

Your subscription is in **Settings → Plan**. **Manage** there opens the billing portal, where you change your plan or payment method, or cancel. Below it, **Invoices** lists your payments.

Stripe checkout and the billing portal return you to your Tamery account or Billing page.

If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

For Enterprise pricing or help with a deployment, use **Contact us** on the [pricing page](https://tamery.app/home#pricing).
