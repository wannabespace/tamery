Pro includes AI chat every month, up to what the subscription costs in model usage. Past that, the chat spends **AI credits**: dollars you add to your account, charged at what the model costs us, with no markup. Credits never expire. They come with Pro; on Free the chat is not available.

Open [AI credits](https://tamery.app/account/credits) in your account to see how much of the month's included amount is used, your credit balance, and your top-ups.

## Add credits

Click **Add credits**, pick $10, $25, $50 or $100, or type an amount from $5 to $1,000, and pay through Stripe. The credits show up a moment after the payment goes through.

## Top up automatically

Turn on **Top up automatically** and set two amounts: when your credits fall below the first, Tamery adds the second, charging the card you paid with. The chat never stops halfway through an answer to wait for a payment.

If a charge fails, auto top-up turns itself off and the page says so. Check your card under **Billing**, then turn it back on.

## When credits run out

With the included amount used and no credits left, the chat stops before its next answer and offers **Add credits**. Everything else in Tamery keeps working.

The month's included amount starts over with each monthly payment. On a yearly plan, it covers the last 30 days.
