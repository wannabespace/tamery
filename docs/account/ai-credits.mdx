Pro includes **$5 of AI** every month, in model usage: the chat, dictation, AI edits and fixes, completions in the Query editor, AI filters and connection diagnosis all spend from it. Past that, AI spends **AI credits**: dollars you add to your account, spent at what the model costs us. Each top-up adds a 5% fee to what you pay; a $10 top-up costs $10.50. Credits never expire. They come with Pro; on Free, AI has its weekly limits instead. With [your own AI key](/account/ai-key), your provider bills the chat and it spends no credits.

Open **Settings → AI** to see how much of the month's included amount is used, your credit balance, your top-ups, and your [usage](#usage).

## Add credits

Click **Add credits** next to **This month**, pick $10, $25, $50 or $100, or type an amount from $10 to $1,000, and pay that amount plus the 5% fee through Stripe. The credits show up a moment after the payment goes through.

## Top up automatically

Turn on **Top up automatically** and set two amounts in whole dollars: when your credits fall below the first, Tamery adds the second, charging the card of your last top-up, plus the 5% fee. The first amount must be lower than the second, and you need one top-up before you can turn it on. The chat never stops halfway through an answer to wait for a payment.

If a charge fails, auto top-up turns itself off and the page says so. Check your card under **Settings → Plan → Manage**, then turn it back on.

## When credits run out

With the included amount used and no credits left, AI stops before its next request: the chat offers **Add credits**, and AI edits, fixes, filters and completions say the budget is spent. Adding [your own AI key](/account/ai-key) keeps the chat going. Everything else in Tamery keeps working.

A request that is already running finishes. What it costs past your credits leaves your balance below zero, and AI stays off, even in a new month, until a top-up covers it. A refunded or disputed top-up takes its credits back the same way.

Tamery's AI works on up to four of your requests at once, chat answers and other AI actions together. A fifth asks you to wait until one of them finishes.

The 7-day trial includes the same $5. Each account gets one trial; subscribing again starts the paid plan right away.

The month's included amount starts over with each monthly payment. On a yearly plan, it covers the last 30 days.

## Usage

The **Usage** section lists your AI requests by month, on Free too. It shows all time by default; pick a date range to narrow it down. Requests made with [your own AI key](/account/ai-key) get their own row, marked **Your key**, with an estimated cost: your provider bills them, so they never count toward the included amount or spend credits.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Month** | The month the requests were made. |
| **Requests** | AI calls made from your account. |
| **Input tokens** | Text and context sent to the model, including cached input. |
| **Output tokens** | Text the model wrote back. |
| **Cost** | What those requests cost us at the model provider. **Unknown** when the provider did not report it. |

**Cost** is there so you can see where the tokens go. It is not an invoice: AI past the included amount spends credits at this cost, and your subscription and payments are under **Settings → Plan**.
