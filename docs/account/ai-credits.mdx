Pro includes **$5 of AI** every month, in model usage: the chat, dictation, AI edits and fixes, completions in the Query editor, AI filters and connection diagnosis all spend from it. Past that, AI spends **AI credits**: dollars you add to your account, spent at what the model costs us. Each top-up adds a 10% fee to what you pay; a $10 top-up costs $11. Credits never expire. They come with Pro; on Free, AI has its weekly limits instead. With [your own AI key](/account/ai-key), your provider bills you and credits aren't spent.

Open [AI credits](https://tamery.app/account/credits) in your account to see how much of the month's included amount is used, your credit balance, and your top-ups.

## Add credits

Click **Add credits**, pick $10, $25, $50 or $100, or type an amount from $10 to $1,000, and pay that amount plus the 10% fee through Stripe. The credits show up a moment after the payment goes through.

## Top up automatically

Turn on **Top up automatically** and set two amounts: when your credits fall below the first, Tamery adds the second, charging the card you paid with, plus the 10% fee. The chat never stops halfway through an answer to wait for a payment.

If a charge fails, auto top-up turns itself off and the page says so. Check your card under **Billing**, then turn it back on.

## When credits run out

With the included amount used and no credits left, AI stops before its next request: the chat offers **Add credits**, and AI edits, fixes, filters and completions say the budget is spent. Adding [your own AI key](/account/ai-key) also keeps AI going. Everything else in Tamery keeps working.

The 7-day trial includes the same $5. Each account gets one trial; subscribing again starts the paid plan right away.

The month's included amount starts over with each monthly payment. On a yearly plan, it covers the last 30 days.
