Open [AI usage](https://tamery.app/account/ai-usage) in your account. It shows all time by default; pick a date range to narrow it down. Each row is one month.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Month** | The month the requests were made. |
| **Requests** | AI calls made from your account. |
| **Input tokens** | Text and context sent to the model, including cached input. |
| **Output tokens** | Text the model wrote back. |
| **Cost** | What those requests cost us at the model provider. **Unknown** when the provider did not report it. |

**Cost** is there so you can see where the tokens go. It is not an invoice, and you are never charged per token. Your subscription and payments are under **Billing** in your account.
