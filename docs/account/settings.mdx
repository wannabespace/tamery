## App preferences

Open the avatar menu and choose **Settings**, or press **Cmd+,** (**Ctrl+,**).

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Desktop only. Start Tamery at login (macOS and Windows), and open database links in Tamery. |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark, or system theme. Transparent windows on macOS. |
| **Keyboard** | Shortcut hints while holding **Cmd** (**Ctrl**), and a searchable list of every shortcut. |
| **MCP** | Desktop only. Agent access, client setup, and activity. See [MCP](/mcp/overview). |

Preferences apply to this device.

The avatar menu also switches the theme, opens the [release notes](https://tamery.app/releases), and has **Support** for writing to us. **Clear cache** wipes what Tamery keeps on this device and reloads. Synced connections and saved queries come back. Query history, layouts, preferences, passwords of **No password** connections, and changes made offline that haven't synced yet do not. Keep it for when something is truly stuck.

## Your account

The avatar menu's **Account** opens [tamery.app/account](https://tamery.app/account) in your browser. There you find:

- **Subscription**, to upgrade or manage Pro
- **Billing**, with your invoices
- **[AI usage](/account/ai-usage)**
- **Settings**, for two-factor authentication, your signed-in sessions, and deleting your account
- **Support**

Under **Settings → Sessions** you can sign out a single device, or all of them except this one with **Sign out other devices**.

To sign out of the app, use the avatar menu. In the CLI, run `tamery logout`.

## Delete your account

Cancel any active subscription first. Deleting the account does not cancel it for you.

Then, on the account page, open **Settings**, click **Delete** under **Account**, type `delete`, and confirm. This removes your Tamery account and its data. We keep a copy of deleted data for 90 days, then erase it for good. The databases you connected to are not touched.
