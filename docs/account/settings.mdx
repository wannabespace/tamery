## App preferences

Click the gear in the title bar, or press **Cmd+,** (**Ctrl+,**).

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Desktop only. Start Tamery at login (macOS and Windows), and open database links in Tamery. |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark, or system theme. Transparent windows on macOS. On desktop, a light, blue, or dark app icon for the Dock (taskbar). |
| **Keyboard** | Shortcut hints while holding **Cmd** (**Ctrl**), and a searchable list of every shortcut. |
| **MCP** | Desktop only. Agent access, client setup, and activity. See [MCP](/mcp/overview). |

Preferences apply to this device.

The avatar menu opens the [release notes](https://tamery.app/releases), and has **Support** for writing to us. **Clear cache** wipes what Tamery keeps on this device and reloads. Synced connections and saved queries come back. Query history, layouts, preferences, passwords of **No password** connections, and changes made offline that haven't synced yet do not. Keep it for when something is truly stuck.

## Your account

The avatar menu's **Account** opens [tamery.app/account](https://tamery.app/account) in your browser. There you find:

- **Subscription**, to upgrade or manage Pro
- **Billing**, with your invoices
- **[AI usage](/account/ai-usage)**
- **Settings**, for two-factor authentication, passkeys, your signed-in sessions, and deleting your account
- **Support**

Under **Settings → Sessions** you can sign out a single device, or all of them except this one with **Sign out other devices**.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN instead of an email code.

1. On the account page, open **Settings**.
2. Under **Passkeys**, click **Add** and confirm with your fingerprint, face, or PIN.
3. Next time, click **Passkey** on the sign-in page.

Your password manager or device keeps the passkey, so it works on every device that syncs it. If adding one asks you to sign in again, sign out and back in, then retry. Signing in with a passkey skips the two-factor code, since unlocking the passkey already proves it's you.

To stop using a passkey, click **Delete** next to it, then remove it from your password manager too.

To sign out of the app, use the avatar menu. In the CLI, run `tamery logout`.

## Security emails

Tamery emails you when:

- someone signs in to your account (after any two-factor check passes), with the device, IP address, and approximate location, including through the desktop app or the CLI, unless the account was created minutes earlier
- Google or GitHub is connected to an existing account, the first time you sign in with it
- a passkey is added
- two-factor authentication is turned on or off
- your account is deleted

If you don't recognize a sign-in, open **Settings → Sessions**, sign that device out, and turn on two-factor authentication. If it came through a passkey, also delete any passkey you don't recognize under **Settings → Passkeys**: signing out leaves the passkey working.

## Delete your account

Cancel any active subscription first. Deleting the account does not cancel it for you. If you are the only owner of a [workspace](/features/workspaces#leave-or-remove-someone) that has other members, make one of them an owner first.

Then, on the account page, open **Settings**, click **Delete** under **Account**, type `delete`, and confirm. This removes your Tamery account and its data. We keep a copy of deleted data for 90 days, then erase it for good. The databases you connected to are not touched.
