## App preferences

Click the gear in the title bar, or press **Cmd+,** (**Ctrl+,**).

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Desktop only. Start Tamery at login (macOS and Windows), and open database links in Tamery. |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark, or system theme. Transparent windows on macOS. On desktop, a light, blue, or dark app icon for the Dock (taskbar). |
| **Keyboard** | Shortcut hints while holding **Cmd** (**Ctrl**), and a searchable list of every shortcut. |
| **MCP** | Desktop only. Agent access, client setup, and activity. See [MCP](/mcp/overview). |
| **Bridge** | Web app only. Connect this browser to `tamery bridge`, or unpair it. See [Tamery CLI](/cli/overview). |

Preferences apply to this device.

The avatar menu opens the [release notes](https://tamery.app/releases), and has **Support** for writing to us.

**Clear cache** in the avatar menu wipes what Tamery keeps on this device and reloads. Synced connections and saved queries come back. Query history, layouts, preferences, passwords of **No password** connections, and changes made offline that haven't synced yet do not.

## Your account

The avatar menu's **Account** opens [tamery.app/account](https://tamery.app/account) in your browser. There you find:

- **Subscription**, to upgrade or manage Pro
- **Billing**, with your invoices
- **[AI usage](/account/ai-usage)**
- **Authentication**, for how you sign in: email, passkeys, Google, GitHub, and two-factor authentication
- **Settings**, for your signed-in sessions and deleting your account
- **Support**

Under **Settings → Sessions** you can sign out a single device, or all of them except this one with **Sign out other devices**.

To sign out of the app, use the avatar menu. In the CLI, run `tamery logout`.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN instead of an email code.

1. On the account page, open **Authentication**.
2. Next to **Passkeys**, click **Add** and confirm with your fingerprint, face, or PIN.
3. Next time, enter your email on the sign-in page and click **Continue with passkey**. To get a code instead, click **Email me a code instead**.

Your password manager or device keeps the passkey, so it works on every device that syncs it. If adding one asks you to sign in again, sign out and back in, then retry. Signing in with a passkey skips the two-factor code, since unlocking the passkey already proves it's you.

Each password manager that syncs needs only one passkey, so adding a second one there is refused. Security keys and passkeys kept on one device can each have their own, so you can register a backup. **Rename** tells them apart.

To stop using a passkey, click **Delete** next to it, then remove it from your password manager too.

## Google and GitHub

Under **Authentication**, click **Connect** next to Google or GitHub to sign in with it too. The account must use the same email as your Tamery account. **Disconnect** removes it, and signing in with an email code keeps working.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication asks for a second step after an email code, Google, or GitHub.

1. On the account page, open **Authentication**.
2. Next to **Authenticator app**, click **Set up** and scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
3. Enter the code from the app, then save your backup codes. They are only shown once.

At sign-in, enter the code from your app or click **Continue with passkey** if you've added one. If you lose your app, click **Use a backup code**.

Under **More options** you can **Regenerate** your backup codes, which stops the old ones from working, or **Disable** two-factor authentication.

## Security emails

Tamery emails you when:

- someone signs in to your account (after any two-factor check passes), with the device, IP address, and approximate location, including through the desktop app or the CLI, unless the account was created minutes earlier
- Google or GitHub is connected to an existing account, either from **Authentication** or the first time you sign in with it
- a passkey is added
- two-factor authentication is turned on or off
- your backup codes are regenerated
- your account is deleted

If you don't recognize a sign-in, open **Settings → Sessions**, sign that device out, and turn on two-factor authentication. If it came through a passkey, also delete any passkey you don't recognize under **Authentication**: signing out leaves the passkey working.

## Delete your account

Cancel any active subscription first. Deleting the account does not cancel it for you. If you are the only owner of a [workspace](/features/workspaces#leave-or-remove-someone) that has other members, make one of them an owner first.

Then, on the account page, open **Settings**, click **Delete** under **Account**, type `delete`, and confirm. This removes your Tamery account and its data. We keep a copy of deleted data for 90 days, then erase it for good. The databases you connected to are not touched.
